What Beacon offers

A unified workspace for the modern CS student.

Most students juggle six tools to do one job. Beacon collapses that into a single, focused environment - built around academic, technical, and career growth.

Academic Task Dashboard

Centralize assignments, exams, and project deadlines with clear visibility of academic priorities.

Technical Skill Tracking

Define learning goals and monitor progress across programming languages, frameworks, and dev tools.

Career Preparation

Structured guidance on career pathways, internships, and industry-aligned skill development.

Academic Support Corner

Motivational reassurance, study organization, and workload management to sustain healthy habits.

Secure Private Notes

Record reflections, study plans, and personal learning notes in a private encrypted space.

Authentication & Security

Industry-grade authentication ensures protected access and trusted personal data storage.

The problem

Computer Science students balance complex academic requirements against rapidly evolving industry expectations. Existing solutions are fragmented - task managers, skill trackers, and career resources rarely speak to each other.

Beacon closes that gap. A centralized dashboard, personalized goal tracking, and structured academic guidance combine into one platform that supports planning, technical growth, and professional readiness across every academic stage.
